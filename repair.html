Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical repair take?
Most common repairs — like screen replacements and battery swaps — are completed within 30 to 60 minutes. More complex issues like water damage or board-level repairs may take 1–5 business days. We'll always give you a time estimate upfront.
Do you use genuine parts?
Yes. We only use genuine OEM or OEM-grade parts sourced from authorized distributors. This ensures your device maintains its performance, water resistance (where applicable), and resale value. We never use cheap aftermarket components.
Is there a warranty on repairs?
Absolutely. Every repair comes with our 90-day warranty covering both parts and labor. If the same issue recurs within that period, we'll fix it again at no charge. Premium Restore package includes a 1-year extended warranty.
Will I lose my data during repair?
In almost all cases, no. Most hardware repairs don't affect your data. However, we always recommend backing up your device before any repair as a precaution. For software repairs or data recovery, we'll discuss data preservation options with you beforehand.
Do you offer pickup and delivery?
Yes! For repairs over $99, we offer free pickup and delivery within the city limits. Just select the option when booking, and we'll arrange a convenient time to collect and return your device.
What if my device can't be repaired?
If we determine your device is beyond repair, we'll let you know immediately — no charge for the diagnosis. We can also help you recover your data and offer trade-in credit toward a new device if you'd like.
