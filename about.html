Our Journey

How We Started

2018

The Idea

Frustrated by overpriced phones and confusing repair options, we started MobiCraft from a small garage with one simple goal: make tech honest and accessible.

2019

First Store Opens

Our first physical location opened in downtown NY. Within six months, we'd served over 5,000 customers and built a reputation for fair prices and expert advice.

2021

Repair Lab Launch

We launched our certified repair lab, offering same-day screen replacements, battery swaps, and water damage recovery — all with genuine parts.

2024

50,000+ Customers

Today, we've helped over 50,000 customers upgrade, repair, and enjoy their devices. And we're just getting started.
